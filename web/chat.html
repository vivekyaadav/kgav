Ask the graph.

Questions are answered from retrieved edges, not from the model's memory. The model only narrates evidence that was already assembled, and an answer that fails verification against that evidence is withheld rather than shown with a warning. Requests for a shortlist of new candidates are refused by design — the graph cannot rank for most viruses, and the refusal says so with the measurement behind it.
