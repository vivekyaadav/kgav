**"Antiviral activity edges" is the predicate's name, not a description of
its contents.** `HAS_ANTIVIRAL_ACTIVITY_AGAINST` means *this compound was
measured against this virus*, and over 29,781 such edges
`kgav.labels.classify` reads:

| verdict | edges | share |
|:---|---:|---:|
| inactive | 23,803 | 79.9% |
| active | 5,426 | 18.2% |
| undecidable | 552 | 1.9% |

Three quarters of this predicate is evidence that a compound does **not**
work — the most valuable part of it, because measured inactives are the
negatives every evaluation here depends on, and also what makes the name
misleading to anyone reading it from outside. They are not routed to
`MEASURED_INACTIVE_AGAINST` because the cut is a protocol parameter:
`hard_negatives.py --inactive-above-nm` varies it and records the value
used, and routing by verdict at ingest would put an evaluation threshold
into the graph's topology.

**HCoV-HKU1 is the extreme case and is worth naming rather than
leaving in a node count**: it carries 0 dependency,
restriction and antiviral edges between them. It is in the register and
answers no question.

### Why the viruses with no dependency factor have none

Not one reason but three, and the distinction matters:

- **No screen in this ORCS release.** DENV-1, DENV-3, DENV-4 and Yellow
  fever. `src/kgav/orcs.py` matches dengue serotype 2, Zika, West Nile and
  yellow fever by pattern; of the human screens it reads, eleven name a
  flavivirus and seven pass `is_virus_resistance_screen`. A yellow fever
  pattern exists and no yellow fever screen survives the gate.
- **No genome-wide screen in the literature at all.** JEV and TBEV. That is
  a limit of the field: no ingest and no curation reaches it.
- **Coronaviruses with no screen reaching them.** SARS-CoV and HCoV-HKU1.

The `curated` layer is declared for exactly this gap and is
`curated` — nothing matches data/raw/curated_screens/*.tsv — so it closes none of it on this release.

## One strain stands for each flavivirus

The register names, per virus, the single accession whose mature-peptide
chains the graph carries. Seven of nine surveyed flaviviruses are curated
under a **strain** taxon rather than the species, so a lineage-walking lookup
would have "worked" for all of them and left no record of which strain it
picked. Declaring it makes the choice auditable; it does not make one strain
representative of a serotype.

## Most drug–target edges do not say which direction

16 predicates carry edges and `TARGETS` is the
host-directed channel's entry point. On this release
10,177 of 17,635 `TARGETS` edges state no direction
— 57.7%, down from 84.5% before the Guide to
PHARMACOLOGY layer. ("No direction" includes the explicit `unknown` the
qualifier carries where a source states none, which is most of them.) "This drug targets a dependency factor" therefore
cannot always become "inhibiting it should block infection", and M2's
`direction = dependency` constraint silently excludes the undirected ones.

## The analysable set is 15.8% of the molecules

The host-directed channels can only reach a compound carrying a human target,
and most of the library does not have one.

Of 25,243 small molecules, **3,977**
carry a human `TARGETS` edge. The 21,266 without one are
**479** approved, **961** clinical,
**35** phase 1, **89** preclinical

**1,440** is the ceiling on what one could add — not
21,266 — because a preclinical compound or one of unknown phase is
not a repurposing candidate however well it scores, so the untouched
remainder that would matter is the approved and clinical part of it.

The cold-start similarity layer exists for the other half of the same
problem: compounds that carry a measured activity and no other edge at all,
so no metapath reaches them in either direction.

## An AUC above chance is not an expected hit rate

AUC is the probability that a randomly chosen active outranks a randomly
chosen inactive: a statement about the **whole ordering**. A screen never sees
the whole ordering; it sees the top of a list. The two can disagree
completely, and here they do. On the one population matched by assay rather
than by source, the head of the ranking holds **no actives at all** once each
candidate's nearest known actives are withheld, under either registered
control — while the cell's AUC clears its alpha either way.

So a row on a shortlist is the claim that a compound **resembles** a known
active, with the known active named. `make hit-rate` measures the head at
three exclusion strengths and labels itself post-hoc.

## A Tanimoto is not readable until you know what a failure scores

Every shortlist row says "this resembles that, at this Tanimoto", and a
Tanimoto has no absolute meaning. The median resemblance a
**screened-and-inactive** compound of the same virus reaches against its own
known actives runs from **0.2115** on MERS-CoV to
**0.7264** on HCoV-OC43 — a factor of
3.4. The same 0.52 is therefore near the top of one cell's
failures and under the median of another's, so a flat display threshold is
wrong in both directions at once.

So `scripts/repurposing_candidates.py` measures that distribution per virus,
scoring each known inactive exactly as it scores a candidate, and reads two
things off it. Its median is a **floor**: a candidate below it resembles the
known actives less than half the compounds already screened against this
virus and failed, and is not shown. Every row that *is* shown carries its own
position in the distribution — the `>fail` column — so "0.52" becomes "better
than 77% of this virus's failures".

This is not a prediction, not a hit rate and not a second protocol: no score
changes, and the cell's registered verdict still decides whether any list is
printed. It is the one yardstick on the page in the same units as the score.

**The threshold was not bent until the disliked row fell out.** The review of 2026-11-19 named TEMAZEPAM on Yellow fever as tail noise. That cell's own failures have a median of 0.3008, so the cell's own standard keeps the row, and it is still on the list with 77% of this virus's failures beside it.

## A similarity score does not know what a compound is for

FLUORESCEIN — an ophthalmic dye — was printed under "approved drugs,
orderable now" for two flaviviruses, resembling the food colourant
ERYTHROSINE. The ranking is max-Tanimoto and cannot tell a therapy from a
contrast agent, so three things travel with each row, and they are
deliberately three different mechanisms:

- **Dropped and named.** A compound **every one** of whose ATC classes is
  diagnostic, contrast, radiotracer or otherwise non-therapeutic
  (`S01J` (ophthalmological diagnostic agents), `V04` (diagnostic agents), `V07` (all other non-therapeutic products), `V08` (contrast media), `V09` (diagnostic radiopharmaceuticals)) is not a repurposing candidate in any sense:
  42 compounds in the pool. It is *every* class and not
  *any* — TOLBUTAMIDE is a provocation test and a sulfonylurea, as are FOLIC
  ACID, DEXTROSE, SORBITOL and GONADORELIN.
- **Labelled and kept.** 205 antineoplastics are a confound to
  declare, not a reason to withhold a row: a cytotoxic nucleoside resembles a
  nucleoside antiviral partly because it *is* a nucleoside, and in a
  cell-based assay it lowers the viral readout by killing the cell.
  219 withdrawn compounds are labelled too and never filtered
  on — ChEMBL's `withdrawn_flag` reads 1 for CODEINE and THALIDOMIDE.
- **Counted as unchecked.** **2,238 of 25,243
  molecules in the release** resolve to no ChEMBL record by id or by
  InChIKey, so their role could not be checked at all. Unchecked is not
  clean. The InChIKey route is not a nicety: 17 of the
  diagnostic agents carry no chembl_id in the release — phenol red,
  indocyanine green, sulfobromophthalein and most of the iodinated contrast
  series — so a filter reading the chembl_id column alone would have missed
  exactly the dyes it exists for.

## The graph's own channels are at chance

0 of 8 metapath channels has a 95%
interval excluding 0.5 on the plate-matched population
(none). The ensemble clears, and
0.5578 of its 0.5666 is its coverage asymmetry
rather than its ranking. A channel reaching fewer than
10 measured actives is reported as **not evaluable**
rather than with a number, because its AUC describes the imputed floor.

## Prospectively, two cells are measured getting it backwards

2 of 10 cells clear **below** chance
prospectively: West Nile, Zika. That is not a null — it is the scorer,
trained on what was known and asked to rank what came next, putting the
actives last. Both cells clear **above** chance cross-sectionally, so a
cross-sectional verdict alone does not license a shortlist, and the read-out
refuses them by name.

## No prospective cell has a usable comparator

0 of 10 cells have a degree
baseline reaching at least 20% of the compounds
being ranked — the pre-cutoff training graph reaches 0.3%–6.5% of them. The
registered decision rule's second clause is therefore satisfied by nothing,
and the outcome is reported as *measurable, not comparable*, which is the
outcome the registration said to expect.

## The clinical axis contradicts the chemistry

0.4087 [0.3734, 0.4440], clearing
**below** chance, against degree at
0.7751. Whether a compound reached a trial is predicted by
how well studied it is. This is the only independent label axis in the
repository.

## The pathway route is a null, with its power stated

44 tests at 100,000 degree-matched permutations, and
**8 viruses have no cell in the host-factor channel**: DENV-1, DENV-3, DENV-4, HCoV-HKU1, JEV, SARS-CoV, TBEV, Yellow fever, because a virus with no `HOST_FACTOR_FOR` edge has no dependency-factor set to overlap. 2 have no interactome cell (DENV-4, Yellow fever), and **2 are missing from both** (DENV-4, Yellow fever) — the same pair the agent refuses to rank, arrived at independently. A null
result is only a finding if the reader knows what would have survived, so the
minimum detectable effect is published per cell. Failing to detect a 2×
excess is not evidence against a 2× excess when the test needed 7×.

## The labels are one kind of measurement at one threshold

Every AUC in this repository but one rests on
`HAS_ANTIVIRAL_ACTIVITY_AGAINST` alone — one predicate derived from in-vitro
concentration-response at one cut. A scorer that recovered the idiosyncrasies
of that screening and nothing else would pass all of them. There are now
271 `IN_TRIAL_FOR` and 1 `TREATS` edges, and
they go the other way.

Within the in-vitro axis: 4,839 label edges carry a potency ChEMBL flagged as
read **outside** its tested dose series (23 of them positives), 157 verdicts
rest on a relation nobody stated, 169 edges carry potency fields that
disagree, and 56.5% of positives at 10 µM are not positive at 1 µM.

## The circularity confound is measured, not removed

779 of 943 compounds carrying both an `INHIBITS` edge
and an antiviral label cite the same publication on both.
58 pairs cannot be withheld at all, because one side
carries no PMID. `--publication-disjoint` removes what it can reach; those
58 it cannot.

## Nine nodes hold two compounds' screening results

<!-- FIGURE: tautomer_conflation -->
The node id is an InChIKey, and standard InChI's mobile-H layer treats
annular tautomers as one compound — `normalize_chemical` canonicalises the
tautomer before keying as well. Every compound node therefore declares
`tautomer_collapsed: true`, and on **9 nodes that policy pools two distinct
NCATS library members**: 18 substances on 9 ids. The clearest pair differs
only in whether a guanidine is written `CCCN=C(N)N` or `CCCNC(N)=N`
(`NCGC00390264` and `NCGC00386242`).

**THESE THREE COUNTS ARE NOT DERIVED AT RENDER TIME, AND THAT IS DECLARED
RATHER THAN HIDDEN.** Recomputing them means re-conflating the raw NCATS
input, which takes minutes — too slow to put inside `make doc2`, which runs
on every commit. They are derived instead by
`tests/test_tautomer_limit_figures.py`, from the release and the raw file,
and that test fails until this paragraph matches. It is the one place in
this folder where the derivation lives in the test rather than in the
generator, and the figures are checked just as often.

**What it costs a reader, measured rather than assumed:** on those nine, an
activity call pools two molecules — and they are not off to one side of the
evaluation. **8 of the 9 carry a `HAS_ANTIVIRAL_ACTIVITY_AGAINST` label and
5 carry a traversable edge**, so a channel can reach them; one of them holds
16 `TARGETS` edges and a similarity edge. Against the labelled population
that is 8 labels, so no published figure moves measurably — but the right
statement is the one with the count in it, not the one that puts them
outside the analysis.

**THE FIRST DRAFT OF THAT PARAGRAPH SAID THE OPPOSITE.** It read "none is
among the compounds any evaluated channel reaches, so no figure depends on
them", written from the assumption that nine tautomer oddities would be
peripheral. They are ordinary screened drugs. The wrong version was the
comfortable one, which is why the counts are asserted against the release
instead of stated in prose.

**It is not fixable by changing the identifier**, which is why it is stated
rather than repaired. Canonical-tautomer SMILES would separate these nine and
split compounds every source treats as one; tautomers are frequently not
separable experimentally either. The alternative is a different identity for
the whole graph to resolve nine nodes out of 25,243.

And the same policy has a consequence at the other end of the scale: a
Tanimoto of 1.0 means the same ECFP4 bits, **not** the same compound. Radius
2 folded into 2,048 bits cannot see a saturated ring's
size, a peptide's length, or a residue's position, so rows at 1.0 against a
different skeleton are marked with their heavy-atom difference rather than
read as identities.

## What the evidence dates mean differs by source

The prospective protocol rests entirely on edge dates, and they are not one
kind of thing. `make data-timeline` separates three:

- **per-edge** — ChEMBL (publication year), UniProt (entry first-public),
  ORCS (screen date);
- **one date on every edge of a source** — STRING, VirHostNet and the NCATS
  plates each carry a single *file release* date, which is not when any fact
  in them was established;
- **undated** — 15,168 edges (3.8%) carry
  `1970-01-01`, the placeholder every ingest writes when it has none:

| source | undated edges |
|:---|---:|
| `infores:virhostnet` | 5,222 |
| `infores:guidetopharmacology` | 3,598 |
| `infores:kgav-computed` | 3,059 |
| `infores:chembl` | 3,006 |
| `infores:interpro` | 158 |
| `infores:chembl|infores:guidetopharmacology` | 109 |
| `infores:mondo` | 16 |

That count rose, and **the rise was a correction rather than a
regression.** Every VirHostNet edge used to carry a blanket release date
applied to all of them regardless of evidence; dating each by the
publication year in its own reference field left those with no parseable
year honestly undated instead of confidently wrong. The Guide to
PHARMACOLOGY edges are undated because GtoP publishes no per-interaction
year, and nothing here can supply one offline — a PMID-to-year index built
from this release is unsound, because PMIDs in it get different years from
different dated edges.

The shape of the date histogram is UniProt's curation history, not a record
of when anything about these drugs was learned.

## What the pathway null could have detected

**A null result is only a finding if the reader knows what would have
survived.** Failing to detect a 2× excess is not evidence against a 2×
excess when the test needed 7×. Minimum detectable effect is the smallest
observed count whose Poisson upper tail falls below the corrected threshold,
as a multiple of the degree-matched expectation.

**8 rows rest on an expectation below ten and carry no
statistical weight**: JEV (interactome: 1 observed, 0.83 expected), West Nile (interactome: 4 observed, 0.91 expected), West Nile (pathway: 0 observed, 2.51 expected), DENV-3 (interactome: 6 observed, 4.43 expected), TBEV (interactome: 4 observed, 4.62 expected), DENV-2 (pathway: 2 observed, 6.28 expected), DENV-1 (interactome: 11 observed, 7.13 expected), HCoV-NL63 (pathway: 10 observed, 7.39 expected).

They **must not be read as depletion.** A ratio computed on an expectation of
three is one Poisson draw, and a ratio of 0.00 on an expectation of 2.5 is
the single most likely observation under a null of no effect. **They must not
be read as depletion.** What they establish is scope — that these viruses
were examined — and nothing else.

**Eight of the twenty-two need a 2.4x excess or more before any observation could clear the bar**, and **Six of the twenty-two downward tests have no rejection region at all.** Under a Poisson background with mean *m* the most
extreme downward observation is zero, with probability exp(−*m*); where that
exceeds the corrected alpha, no observation whatsoever can clear the bar.
Those tests are in the correction family and cannot contribute a finding to
it, which makes the ones that can harder to pass for no return. Dropping
them is defensible — emptiness depends on the matched expectation and not on
any outcome, so it is not choosing tests after seeing them — but the smaller
family belongs in a registration that does not exist yet, and it is left
alone until it does.

## Ten diseases for sixteen viruses, which is not the reason anything is empty

The release carries 10 `Disease` nodes for
16 viruses, because four dengue serotypes share one MONDO
id and four coronaviruses share another, and every register entry carries
one.

This is recorded because the **stated cause** of the empty clinical labels
was wrong for ten days. It read "13 of 16 register entries carry
`mondo: null`, which is what leaves the two labels empty"; the curation had
already been done, the audit of 2026-11-07 recorded that it had, and the
sentence survived because the test underneath it checked that the predicate
*names* were present rather than that the explanation was true. **A presence
check cannot fail on a wrong explanation.** The real cause was narrower:
ChEMBL's `drug_indication` table was on disk and was never queried. It is
queried now — 271 `IN_TRIAL_FOR` and 1
`TREATS` edges — and the result is the null in the section above.

## Questions this graph cannot be asked at all

The schema declares more than the release holds, and a reader has to be told
which questions are therefore unavailable rather than merely unanswered.

**4 declared node classes have no nodes**:
`CellLine`, `Publication`, `Variant`, `VirusStrain`. **8 declared predicates have no
edges**: `COMBINED_WITH`, `CONFERS_RESISTANCE_TO`, `HAS_STRAIN`, `HAS_VARIANT`, `OCCURS_IN`, `PREDICTED_INDICATION`, `SUBCLASS_OF`, `SUBCLASS_OF_DISEASE`.

The consequence worth naming is the **resistance axis** — `VirusStrain`,
`Variant`, `CONFERS_RESISTANCE_TO`, `OCCURS_IN`, `HAS_VARIANT`, `HAS_STRAIN`
— which is entirely absent. No claim of the form *this mutation confers
resistance to this drug* can be made or checked here.

This list is derived, and that matters: it said `TREATS` and `IN_TRIAL_FOR`
were empty for ten days after the indications layer filled them, because the
test underneath it checked that the predicate names were present rather than
that the explanation was true.

## Two inputs carry a reconstructed provenance record

`make check-raw` verifies every raw input. The host layer — the human
proteome and STRING, feeding over three-quarters of the edges — carries a
record reconstructed after the fact: a checksum, with the URL and retrieval
date null, because the files were placed by hand and neither is recoverable.
Several coronavirus proteomes are in the same state.

A reconstructed record makes drift detectable from now on. **It does not
make the build reproducible from a clean machine**; that needs a fetcher for
those inputs, which does not exist.

## What the agent will not do, as a consequence

- refuse to rank **novel** chemical matter for any virus — compounds enter
  screening because they are novel, which is what a prior-evidence graph
  cannot represent;
- refuse any ranking question for a virus with no route at all, because an
  empty ranking reads as "no compound looks promising" and the difference
  between finding nothing and being unable to look is the whole distinction;
- attach a cell-context caveat to every host-directed path, because
  chloroquine's route through SIGMAR1 is real, well replicated, and led the
  field astray in 2020.
